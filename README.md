# Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead — Supernatural & Vampire Mod Suite (Latest Experimental 2026+)

A curated collection of lore-accurate, fully tested, and mechanically integrated supernatural mods for Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead (CDDA Latest Experimental 2026+).

Built on and expanding upon **Xedra Evolved** and **Magiclysm**.

---

## Included Mods

### 1. The Elder Vampire (`elder_vampire`)
Play as an ancient vampire progenitor of terrifying antiquity.
- **Vampiric Blood Arts**: Full progression through Xedra Evolved vampirism stages with ancient vitae.
- **Siring & Thralls**: Feed on humans and turn them into fledglings or thralls bound to your blood.
- **Supernatural Combat**:
  - *Supernatural Breach Kick* (110 bash damage): Pulverize reinforced doors and masonry while hurling foes backward.
  - *Supersonic Kinetic Throw* (18 tiles range): Flick coins, knives, and severed limbs at Mach 3 with devastating armor penetration.
  - *Decapitation & Neck Snapping*: Instantly execute vulnerable foes and claim severed limb trophies.
- **Five TVD Occult Relics**:
  - **Mikaelson Blood Chalice**: 1,000 mL insulated heirloom chalice that never spoils blood and grants +20% bonus vitae.
  - **Silver Dagger of Desiccation**: Dipped in white oak ash; paralyzes and stuns supernatural entities on hit.
  - **Lapislazuli Moonstone Talisman**: Night vision, mana regen, and occult damage protection.
  - **Papa Tunde's Sacrificial Blade**: Siphons pain and vitae from victims into unholy strength.
  - **Gilbert Agony Device**: Pocket brass clockwork mechanism emitting sonic pulses that disable nearby vampires.

### 2. Elijah Mikaelson — The Noble Original (`elijah_mikaelson`)
Play as Elijah Mikaelson, the Noble Original vampire.
- **Thousand-Year-Old Immortality**: Needs no food or water, only blood. Undying regeneration that closes wounds in seconds.
- **Original Apex Stride (~100 km/h)**: Flawless dodge and blazing velocity burning 5 mL blood per 10 turns.
- **7 Signature Original Combat Abilities**:
  - *Original Breach Kick* (200 bash damage): Catastrophic blunt force. Knocks Elder Vampires backward 7 tiles, leaving them downed for 10 seconds coughing dark vitae.
  - *Savage Body Hurl* (10 tiles throw range): Grab an adjacent enemy and heave their entire bulk across the room; wall collisions shatter bones and barriers.
  - *Apex Blitz & Neck Snap* (14 tiles blitz): Blur directly to a victim, seize their jaw, and snap their cervical spine. Instant decapitation for creatures under 400 HP.
  - *Corpse Whirlwind*: Centrifugal 360-degree battering ram clearing rooms of hostile swarms.
  - *Supersonic Kinetic Throw* (25 tiles range): Propel improvised inventory items (coins, ammo, ball bearings, severed limbs) past Mach 3.
  - *Surgical Limb Tear & Spine-Breaker*: Dismember enemy arms and legs or stomp lower spines into powder, permanently disarming and paralyzing foes.
  - *Apex Blood Radar* (45 tiles detection): Track every beating heart, fresh wound, and living entity through walls and total darkness.
- **Lore & Artifacts**: The Red Door in Elijah's mind, daylight ring, New Orleans house with daggered siblings in the crypt, and the unified White Oak Stake.

### 3. Klaus Mikaelson — The Original Hybrid (`klaus_mikaelson`)
Play as Niklaus Mikaelson before and after breaking the curse of the Sun and the Moon.
- **The Original Hybrid**: Thousand-year-old Original vampire combined with untamed werewolf blood.
- **Hybrid Werewolf Combat Arts**:
  - *Hybrid Breach Kick* (200 bash damage): Powered by savage werewolf fury.
  - *Savage Body Hurl*, *Apex Blitz*, *Hybrid Vortex Sweep*, *Mach 3 Kinetic Throw*, *Savage Limb Tear*, and *Apex Werewolf Blood Radar*.
- **The Curse Questline**: Hunt down the moonstone, gather the doppelganger's blood, and unbind the werewolf within to unleash full hybrid form.
- **Unified White Oak Integration**: Vulnerable only to the true White Oak Stake.

### 4. Van Helsing & The Crimson Chalice (`van_helsing`)
Step into the trench coat of the legendary monster hunter Abraham Van Helsing.
- **Hunter Armaments**: Fortified rural safehouse, silver-forged blades, vervain-loaded rapid crossbow, and consecrated stakes.
- **The Crimson Chalice Sanctuary Tavern**: An iconic neutral-ground tavern protected by the ancient Accord of Sanctuary, where vampires, werewolves, and hunters drink side-by-side under strict non-aggression rules.
- **Custom Scenario & Quests**: Travel to the Crimson Chalice, converse with ancient barkeeps, and trade occult intelligence.

### 5. Alpha Werewolf (`xe_alpha_werewolf`)
Embrace primal lycanthropy with the Alpha Werewolf mod.
- **Alpha Werewolf Profession & Traits**: Enhanced regeneration, heightened predatory senses, devastating claw damage, and lunar fury.
- **'Last of the Pack' Scenario**: Start stranded with a battered pickup truck and an ancestral vendetta.
- **Vampire Lord Hunt**: Track down and slaughter the vampire lord who wiped out your pack.

---

## Installation Guide
1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Copy the 5 folders inside `mods/` into your CDDA installation directory under `data/mods/` or `mods/`:
   - `mods/elder_vampire`
   - `mods/elijah_mikaelson`
   - `mods/klaus_mikaelson`
   - `mods/van_helsing`
   - `mods/xe_alpha_werewolf`
3. When creating a new world in CDDA, enable the mods in the world generation menu.
