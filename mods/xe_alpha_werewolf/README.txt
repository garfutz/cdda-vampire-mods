ALPHA WEREWOLF  -  add-on for Xedra Evolved, Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead
Built and tested against: cdda-experimental-2026-09-28-1737

REQUIRES: Xedra Evolved (ships with the game - enable it too).

INSTALL
  1. Unzip. Put the "xe_alpha_werewolf" folder in <Cataclysm folder>/data/mods/
  2. NEW world -> enable Xedra Evolved and "Alpha Werewolf (Xedra Evolved)".
     (New world needed: the mod adds the homestead to the map.)
  3. Scenario tab: "Last of the Pack".  Profession tab: "Alpha Werewolf".
     Don't name the world/character "Test" or "Debug".

PROFESSION "Alpha Werewolf" (8 points, works in any scenario)
  - Xedra Evolved werewolf: Natural Shapeshifter, Lupine Form, War Form, Gifts of the
    Moon (werewolf mana).
  - ALPHA OF THE PEOPLE (unique trait from this mod), always on:
      +4 Strength, +2 Dexterity, +3 Perception, +15 speed, +25 HP, double healing,
      +25% melee damage, 4 points of damage reduction, +400 werewolf mana and faster
      mana regen, more stamina, knockdown resistance, very intimidating.
    At night it gets stronger: another +2 Strength, +10 speed, faster healing again,
    +15% melee damage.  (Night start: Str 14, Dex 10, Per 11, speed 125 before you shift.)
  - ALPHA'S HOWL (new power, 150 werewolf mana, cast with ] ): every hostile within
    8 tiles is stunned for 3 turns and you go into Alpha's Fury for 5 minutes:
    +4 Strength, +25 speed, faster attacks, half pain, extra damage reduction.
  - Eighteen Gifts of the Moon already learned, from all five levels:
      Scent the Prey, Mighty Blow, Among the Birds and Beasts, Running through the
      River, Spider Climb, Go for the Throat, Grasshopper's Leap, Sense the Outsider,
      Shimmerpelt, Partial Shift, Primal Strength, Red-Handed Rage, Thrill of the Hunt,
      Argent Claws, Gift of Lunar Renewal, Lucent Armor, Form of the Primal Hunter,
      and Summon the Shadow Pack (your dead pack answers your howl).
    The rest (Moon's Cold Glow, Gremlinize, Hunter's Haze, Strike the Inner Outsider,
    Mending Rent Veils, Ghostwalker) are yours to earn: you're past the level-5 gate and
    the next Gift comes after a few more unarmed kills of Outsiders.
  - Night Vision, Tough, Pain Resistant, Strong Stomach.
  - Unarmed 7, Dodge 6, Survival 7, Melee 4, Devices 4, Athletics 4 and more; unarmed
    and anatomy proficiencies, butchering, skinning, trapping, driving.
  - Gear: flannel, tank top, jeans, boots, fingerless gloves, the alpha's fang necklace,
    a photo of the pack, hunting knife (sheathed), hatchet, 3 ash stakes and 2 Molotovs
    in the backpack (packed, not wielded: XE only gives werewolf XP for unarmed kills),
    binoculars, lighter, jerky, canteen, and the vampire's blood-inked letter.

SCENARIO "Last of the Pack" (lone start)
  You start inside your off-grid homestead deep in the woods, at night:
    - Log cabin: wood stove, bed, armchair, table, kitchen counter and cupboards
      (flour, sugar, salt, coffee, honey, oil, pots, canning jars), pantry shelves
      (canned food, pemmican, jerky, water), a chest freezer (venison, fish, smoked
      meat, bacon), survival books and seed packets, spare clothes, oil lamp, radio.
    - Yard: stacked cord wood + logs and kindling, splitting stump with an axe, the
      pack's fire ring, a hand-pumped well, a rain tank, smoking rack, kitchen garden.
    - Power: a diesel generator, a 200 L diesel tank, 2 x 20 L + 2 x 10 L diesel cans,
      a 20 L gas can for the truck, a solar unit, extension cables, and tools (saw,
      shovel, hoe, bucket, rope, tarp, fishing rod, duct tape).
      Examine the generator/freezer/solar unit and answer Yes to connect them to the grid.
    - Your battered pickup is on the drive: dented panels, busted glass and lights,
      45% gas - but it starts and drives.
  Every profession gets the werewolf forms here; the Alpha profession fits the story.

MISSION "Avenge the pack: kill Aurelio Voss"
  Given at the start of the scenario (other scenarios: read the blood-inked letter).
  The old hunting cabin is marked on your map (M = missions, m = map). Voss is a
  900 HP, fast vampire lord guarded by renfields, a moroi and vampire bats, with two
  enthralled victims in the cabin. Kill him for +1000 werewolf XP and his signet ring.

MORE HOMESTEAD DETAIL
  Cabin: a chest of pack mementos (photos, pelts, fur blanket, cards, guitar), a trophy
  case of skulls (deer, bear, moose, cougar, a wolf-skull helmet), coat rack with fur
  coat and hat, sofa by the stove, extra bookshelf.  Yard: butcher rack and meathook,
  camp chairs round the fire ring, a picnic table, more log stacks, an archery target,
  flowers.
VOSS'S CABIN
  A pipe organ, a broken mirror, a trophy case of werewolf pelts and skulls (including
  your pack's photo), meathooks, a glass cabinet of wine and a gold chalice, candlelit
  tables, sofas, and two statues flanking the path to the door.

==============================================================
EXPANSION: WREN, THE BONE COUNTESS, AND FULL-MOON NIGHTS
==============================================================
WREN CALLOWAY (companion)
  Voss's cabin now has a chained cellar hatch in the back room.
  Examine it to tear the chain off and free Wren, a werewolf from
  the neighbouring Harlan pack whom Voss kept alive to drink from.
  Ask her to run with you and she follows you as a companion: fast,
  tough, quick to heal, and a strong unarmed fighter.  Talk to her
  about who she is and about Voss's maker.

THE BONE COUNTESS (second boss)
  Killing Voss gives you a letter sealed in black wax and a new
  mission: "The Bone Countess".  Magdalena Szell, the vampire who
  turned Voss, waits in a ruined chapel marked on your map, with two
  fast moroi, two renfields and her bats.  She has 1700 HP, hits hard,
  regenerates and summons more bats during the fight.
  Reward: +2000 werewolf experience and her bone ring.

FULL-MOON NIGHTS
  On a full-moon night the Alpha gets +4 Str, +20 speed, double
  regeneration, +25% melee damage and +200 mana (on top of the
  normal night bonus).  When the full moon rises you get a message
  and a morale boost, and half the time 1-2 rogue werewolves come
  to challenge you for your territory.
