KLAUS MIKAELSON (THE ORIGINAL)  -  Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead mod
Built and tested against: cdda-experimental-2026-09-28-1737

REQUIRES: Xedra Evolved + Magiclysm (both ship with the game - enable them too).
Works alongside the Stefan Salvatore mod.

INSTALL
  1. Unzip. Put the "klaus_mikaelson" folder in <Cataclysm folder>/data/mods/
  2. NEW world -> enable Xedra Evolved, Magiclysm and "Klaus Mikaelson (The Original)".
  3. Scenario "The Originals: Before the Curse Broke", profession tab -> Klaus Mikaelson.
     Don't name the world or character "Test"/"Debug".

THE PROFESSION (10 points, works in any scenario)
  Klaus before the Mystic Falls sacrifice: an Original vampire whose werewolf side is
  still sealed by Esther's curse of the sun and the moon.
  - Xedra Evolved vampirism at full depth: Vigor Mortis, Sanguine Resilience, Predator's
    Senses, Silent as Death, Gleaming Eyes, Sanguine Restoration, Flow Like Blood,
    Predator's Mien, Corpse's Countenance, Blood-Driven Speed, Hypnotic Gaze and
    Dominating Gaze (compulsion - an Original can compel anyone), Incarnadine Energy,
    Fortitude of the Damned, Facultative Breathing.  Blood cravings.
  - THE ORIGINAL: +7 Str, +4 Dex, +4 Per, +30 speed, +50 HP, triple healing, +40% melee
    damage, 7 points of damage reduction, huge stamina, hard to knock down, terrifying.
    A little stronger again at night.  (Start: Str ~20, speed ~135.)
  - ORIGINAL IMMORTALITY:
      * Any body part below 1/3 health heals completely within seconds (once a minute).
      * If any part of you is destroyed, even your head or heart, you die, and a moment
        later rise again fully restored - broken bones and all - and furious (Mikaelson
        Temper for 3 minutes).  No cooldown.  Ordinary stakes, bullets, fire, blades:
        nothing kills you for good.
      * The ONLY thing that can kill Klaus is the white oak stake.  It is locked in the
        strongbox in the Mikaelson house, and no hunter or monster carries one.
  - THE CURSE OF THE SUN AND THE MOON: no wolf form - it's locked away.  On moonlit
    nights outside you feel the wolf pacing its cage.
  - Weaknesses: the sun (wear the ring!), blood cravings, white oak.
  - Powers (cast with ] , paid in stolen blood):
      Original Speed (blur 12 tiles), Feed (bite and drink from humans/animals),
      Rip Out the Heart (90-160 damage), Healing Blood, Mikaelson Temper (rage buff:
      +6 Str, +30 speed, faster attacks, pain ignored).
  - Traits: Always and Forever, Artist's Soul (sketching calms you), Night Vision,
    Good Memory, Pretty.  Melee/Unarmed/Dodge 8, Social 8, knives, archery, tailoring...
  - Gear: charcoal henley, dark field jacket, jeans, boots, layered necklaces, silver ring,
    ORIGINAL'S DAYLIGHT RING (take it off in daylight and you burn), silver dagger
    (white oak ash) in a sheath, hip flask of bourbon, 3 blood bags, leather sketchbook
    + charcoal, a charcoal sketch of Katerina Petrova (1492), phone, messenger bag.

SCENARIO "The Originals: Before the Curse Broke"
  Night, the old Mikaelson house in New Orleans' French Quarter: walled courtyard with a
  statue, Klaus's studio (paintings, canvases, brushes, charcoal, the family portrait),
  bedroom with the MOONSTONE on the desk, a bar of old bourbon, fridges full of blood
  bags, a piano, a strongbox holding the last WHITE OAK STAKE, and the coffin room:
  Elijah, Rebekah, Kol and Finn, each with a silver dagger and a nameplate.

THE HOUSE (furnished)
  Studio: standing mirror, a display case (silver daggers, paintings, a cavalry saber),
  lamps, plants.  Bedroom: mirror, lamp, plants and a Viking chest from home (battle
  axe, throwing axes, fur blanket, gold chalice, candlesticks, history book, gold).
  Study: glass cabinet of bourbon and wine, coffee table with chess set and cigars.
  Coffin room: candlelit table, a display case, plants.  Courtyard: birdbath, flower
  beds, candlelit tables and chairs.  South wing: a dining table, wine cabinet.

==============================================================
EXPANSION: BREAKING THE CURSE, THE SIBLINGS, AND THE HUNTERS
==============================================================
BREAKING THE CURSE
  1. Use ("a") the moonstone from the desk.  Four missions are added,
     all marked on your map:
       - kill the chosen werewolf at an old stone circle
       - kill the chosen vampire in an overgrown crypt
       - kill the coven witch in her shack (she throws witchfire)
       - go to the Petrova house and take the vial of
         doppelganger's blood from the desk
  2. Using the moonstone again shows what's still missing.
  3. When all three sacrifices are made and you carry the blood, use the
     moonstone OUTDOORS AT NIGHT.  The ritual:
       - removes the Curse of the Sun and the Moon
       - grants Lupine Form (XE werewolf shapeshift) and the
         Original Hybrid trait: the sun can't burn you even without
         the ring, and you get more strength, speed, HP and mana
         (more still at night)
       - teaches Hybrid Bite (feeds on humans, and floods vampires
         with lethal werewolf venom) and Call the Hybrids (summons
         three wolves for 30 minutes)

THE DAGGERED SIBLINGS
  Examine a coffin in the coffin room to pull the dagger out.
  Elijah, Rebekah and Finn will follow you if you ask.  Kol needs
  persuading, and if you fail he walks off on his own.  All four are
  Originals: very strong, fast, tough and quick to heal.  You get the
  dagger back each time.

VAMPIRE HUNTERS
  Mikael no longer hunts you.  Instead, from day 5, every day or so
  there's a good chance a squad of 2-3 vampire hunters comes for you.
  They belong to the Order of the Ash Stake, a brotherhood that has
  hunted the Mikaelsons for centuries and is hostile on sight.
  They're Xedra Evolved's vampire hunters, better trained: tougher,
  faster, expert shots with compound crossbows, good with stakes and
  machetes, and wearing Kevlar.  They carry ordinary ash stakes, which
  hurt but can never kill you.  None of them has white oak.

==============================================================
COLLECTIBLE LORE
==============================================================
Letters, journals and photos are now hidden around this mod's locations
(and on some bosses).  Activate one to read it.  The first time you read
each one you get a small morale boost, and some also give a bit of
skill experience.  Reading them again just shows the text.


==============================================================
LATEST CHANGES
==============================================================
  - Klaus no longer needs food or water, only blood.
  - Mikael no longer hunts you.
  - The only thing that can kill Klaus is the white oak stake, locked in
    the strongbox in the study.  No hunter carries one.
  - Elijah is now his own separate mod (Elijah Mikaelson).  In this mod
    he's still Klaus's daggered brother in the coffin room.
  - This mod is separate from the Mystic Falls Crossover.

ORIGINAL BODY (v1.1 fix)
  - Coming back from death now restores every body part, including eyes,
    mouth, hands and feet (before, they stayed BROKEN after a revival,
    which left you blind).  Any broken minor part also regrows on its own.
  - Originals can't starve to death.  If you go without blood you dry out
    to "Corpse-like" (weak, slow, sleepy) but never reach the lethal
    "Eternal Slumber" stage.  Feed to recover.
  - Hunger is permanently zero: blood is the only thing you need.
