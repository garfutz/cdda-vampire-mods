ELIJAH MIKAELSON (THE NOBLE ORIGINAL)  -  Cataclysm: Dark Days Ahead mod
=========================================================================
Requires: Xedra Evolved.  This is a standalone mod: it does not need the
Klaus mod, and it doesn't conflict with it if you use both.

INSTALL
  1. Unzip into Cataclysm's data/mods folder (you get data/mods/elijah_mikaelson).
  2. NEW world -> enable Xedra Evolved and "Elijah Mikaelson (The Noble Original)".
  3. Scenario "Elijah: Family Above All", profession tab -> Elijah Mikaelson.

THE STORY
  A thousand years ago Elijah was a Viking's son in a village in the New
  World, until his mother made her children the first vampires.  Since that
  night he has kept one vow above all others: always and forever.  He has
  stood between Niklaus and the world for ten centuries: Mikael's hunting,
  Katerina's betrayal, the burning of New Orleans, a hundred daggers in a
  hundred backs.  He is courteous, patient and impeccably dressed, and he
  is the most dangerous person in any room.  Behind a red door in his mind
  he keeps the man Niklaus used to send when he needed hearts torn out.

SCENARIO: Elijah: Family Above All
  10 PM in New Orleans with the French Quarter burning.  You start in your
  own house in the Garden District:
    - Parlor: the piano, a fireplace, a chess table, sofa and armchair.
    - Library: walls of books (philosophy, history, biography, novels), a
      writing desk with your old journals and an unsent letter.
    - Dining room: long table, wine cabinet, and a fridge of blood bags.
    - Bedroom: your wardrobe of suits, and a crooked painting of a Norse
      village.  Behind it is a locked safe holding the WHITE OAK STAKE,
      the only thing that can kill you.
    - Crypt: Rebekah, Kol and Finn in their coffins with silver daggers in
      their hearts.  Examine a coffin to wake them: Rebekah and Finn will
      follow you; Kol has to be persuaded and may walk off instead.  Waking
      a sibling lifts your spirits.  They're Originals: very strong, fast,
      tough and quick to heal.

PROFESSION: Elijah Mikaelson (10 points)
  - The Noble Original: +6 Str, +5 Dex, +5 Per, +4 Int, +30 speed, +50 HP,
    triple healing, +35% melee damage, 7 points of damage reduction, huge
    stamina, hard to knock down.  Sharper at night.
  - NO HUNGER, NO THIRST.  Elijah never needs food or water.  The only
    thing he needs is blood (Xedra Evolved's blood meter).
  - Original Immortality: any body part below 1/3 health heals completely
    in seconds (once a minute).  If any part of him is destroyed, even his
    head or heart, he drops for a moment and rises fully healed.  Only the
    white oak stake can kill him.  No hunter carries one.
  - White Oak Bane: carrying the white oak stake makes him uneasy.
  - A Man of His Word: +35 persuasion, +20 intimidation, worse at lying.
  - The Red Door: when his head or torso drops below half health there's a
    50% chance (once an hour at most) his darker self takes over for 2
    minutes: +8 Str, +40 speed, +50% melee damage, faster attacks, no pain.
    Afterwards he feels heavy guilt.
  - Family Above All, Refined Tastes (learns and reads faster), Fast
    Reader, Good Memory, Night Vision, Pretty.
  - The full Xedra Evolved vampire trait set: blood powers, hypnotic gaze,
    dominate, fear gaze, blood heal, no need to breathe, and the sun burns
    him without his daylight ring.
  - Skills: melee 6, unarmed 6, dodge 5, speech 7, first aid 4,
    survival 3, driving 3, cooking 3, pistol 2.

POWERS (cast with ])
  Rip Out the Heart  180-300 damage.  Anything with a heart that's left at
                     250 HP or less dies on the spot with its heart in your
                     hand, and taking a heart heals you and feeds you a
                     little.  (120 ml blood)
  Decapitation       220-360 damage.  Anything with a head that's left at
                     300 HP or less is beheaded outright.  Beheaded zombies
                     stay dead for good.  (200 ml blood)
  Feed               drink from an adjacent creature:
                       humans   - the most blood, plus a morale boost
                       animals  - less blood, keeps the thirst at bay
                       vampires - good blood, and it heals you
                     Zombie blood is dead and can't be drunk.
  Compulsion         a human or vampire fights for you for 5 minutes
  Noble Resolve      10 minutes of better dodging, less pain, more damage
                     and persuasion
  Original Speed     dash 12 tiles
  Healing Blood      heal yourself or an ally

GEAR
  Bespoke three-piece suit, dress shirt, necktie, dress shoes, three silk
  pocket squares, pocket watch, daylight ring, his current journal,
  Niklaus's sketch of him, the "Always and Forever" vow in Rebekah's hand,
  a silver dagger in a sheath, three blood bags, a phone and a bag.

VAMPIRE HUNTERS: The Order of the Ash Stake
  From day 5, every day or so there's a good chance a squad of 2-3 hunters
  comes for you, hostile on sight.  They're Xedra Evolved's vampire
  hunters, better trained: tougher, faster, expert shots with compound
  crossbows, good with stakes and machetes, and wearing Kevlar.  Their ash
  stakes hurt, but they can't kill an Original.

MISSION: The Petrova line
  The last Petrova's house outside the city holds a vial of doppelganger
  blood, the thing Niklaus needs to break his curse.  Get there first.

LORE (activate to read; first read gives a small morale or skill bonus)
  Journal from the year 1002, the unsent letter to Katerina (1492), the
  New Orleans journal (1919), a page about the red door, Niklaus's sketch
  of you, and the vow.

ORIGINAL BODY (v1.1 fix)
  - Coming back from death now restores every body part, including eyes,
    mouth, hands and feet (before, they stayed BROKEN after a revival,
    which left you blind).  Any broken minor part also regrows on its own.
  - Originals can't starve to death.  If you go without blood you dry out
    to "Corpse-like" (weak, slow, sleepy) but never reach the lethal
    "Eternal Slumber" stage.  Feed to recover.
  - Hunger is permanently zero: blood is the only thing you need.
